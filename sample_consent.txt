INFORMED CONSENT FORM — SAMPLE RESEARCH STUDY

Purpose: The purpose of this study is to evaluate a new treatment for chronic pain.

Procedure: If you choose to participate, you will receive the study medicine through a vein every two weeks for twelve weeks. The study team will ask about your symptoms at each visit.

Potential benefits: The treatment may improve pain symptoms, but there is no guarantee that you will benefit. You may not receive a direct benefit from taking part.

Risks and side effects: Possible adverse events include nausea, dizziness, bleeding, and infection. Some risks are not fully understood. Serious or life-threatening complications may occur. Tell the study team about new symptoms.

Alternatives: You may choose standard treatment, another available treatment, or no treatment. Your doctor can explain these options to you.

Monitoring and follow-up: Your health will be monitored during treatment and during follow-up visits. You should tell the study team about new symptoms or concerns.

Voluntary participation: Taking part is voluntary. You may refuse to participate or withdraw at any time without affecting your usual medical care. You may ask questions before or during the study.

Privacy: The study team will keep your information confidential and will limit access to authorized study staff.
